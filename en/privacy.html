Privacy Policy

Quick summary

Our image-to-PDF tool runs entirely inside your browser. We never upload your images or files to any server, we don't store them, and we never even see them. All processing happens on your device using JavaScript in the browser.

What we collect

  • Your files: we don't collect or upload them. They stay on your device.
  • Usage data: we may use general analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to see visit counts and page usage. This data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Local storage: we save your preferences (language, dark mode) in localStorage on your device only.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies for analytics and advertising. When you visit, third parties (such as Google AdSense) may set cookies to show relevant ads. You can disable them from your browser settings.

Advertising

We rely on Google AdSense ads to cover hosting costs. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits. For more information, see Google's advertising policy.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. You can always clear your browser's local data to remove your preferences.

Contact

For any privacy questions, reach us via our contact page
