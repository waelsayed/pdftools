    Why convert images to PDF?

    Students photograph lecture slides, handwritten assignments and textbook pages every day. Those images end up scattered across a phone gallery. Merging them into a single PDF makes them easy to share, print, and keep in order.

    Common use cases

    • Combine lecture photos into a single study PDF.
    • Submit handwritten homework as a PDF.
    • Group scanned book pages for revision.
    • Bundle photographed receipts or documents.

    Why privacy matters

    Most online converters upload your images to a server first, then return the PDF. That means your personal or academic documents sit on machines you know nothing about. Our tool works differently: conversion runs entirely in your browser, and your files are never transmitted. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

    How to use it

    1. Drag images into the drop zone, or click to choose them.
    2. Reorder by dragging; rotate or delete as needed.
    3. Pick page size, margins, and quality.
    4. Click convert — the PDF downloads instantly.
