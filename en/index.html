    Why convert images to PDF?

    Students photograph lecture slides, handwritten assignments and textbook pages every day. Those images end up scattered across a phone gallery. Merging them into a single PDF makes them easy to share, print, and keep in order.

    Common use cases

    • Combine lecture photos into a single study PDF.
    • Submit handwritten homework as a PDF.
    • Group scanned book pages for revision.
    • Bundle photographed receipts or documents.

    Why privacy matters

    Most online converters upload your images to a server first, then return the PDF. That means your personal or academic documents sit on machines you know nothing about. Our tool works differently: conversion runs entirely in your browser, and your files are never transmitted. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

    How to use it

    1. Drag images into the drop zone, or click to choose them.
    2. Reorder by dragging; rotate or delete as needed.
    3. Pick page size, margins, and quality.
    4. Click convert — the PDF downloads instantly.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the image-to-PDF converter free?

    Yes, the tool is completely free with no limits on the number of images or files, and no account or sign-up required.

    Are my images uploaded to any server?

    No. Conversion happens entirely inside your browser using modern web technologies — your images never leave your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after opening the page and conversion will still work.

    Which image formats are supported?

    We support JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and BMP, plus HEIC photos from iPhone, which are converted automatically in the browser.

    Can I reorder or rotate images before converting?

    Yes. Reorder images by drag and drop, rotate any image 90° from the preview or the thumbnail, and delete what you don't need before creating the file.

    How do I control the output PDF file size?

    From settings, choose the image resolution (large, medium or original) and compression quality. Medium resolution with high quality gives a lightweight file ideal for sharing on WhatsApp and Telegram.
